About Eisy Myanmar

Secure virtual cards · USDT · P2P for Myanmar

Our platform

Eisy Myanmar is a digital finance platform built for people in Myanmar who need reliable access to global payments. We provide secure virtual cards, USDT wallet services, and peer-to-peer (P2P) USDT/MMK trading — so users can move money with clarity, speed, and stronger safeguards.

From card issuance and reloads to escrow-backed P2P trades, the product is designed around practical everyday use: transparent fees, identity-aware security where required, and a calm interface that works on both desktop and mobile.

  • Virtual cards — issue and manage cards for online payments
  • USDT wallets — deposit, hold, and move USDT with clear ledger history
  • P2P Express — trade USDT and MMK with structured escrow flows

Founder / Creator

Pyae Phyo Naing

Eisy Myanmar was founded by Pyae Phyo Naing, who built the platform to make trustworthy digital finance more accessible in Myanmar. The guiding priorities are simple and non-negotiable: protect user funds and data, design for real-world security threats, and reduce friction for people who need modern payment tools.

That means shipping carefully, communicating clearly, and treating trust as a product feature — not a slogan. The long-term goal is financial accessibility with the same expectations of safety and professionalism users should have from any serious financial service.
