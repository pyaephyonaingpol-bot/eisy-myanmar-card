Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Eisy Myanmar, we strive to provide a secure and instant virtual card experience. Please read our refund rules carefully before initiating any transactions.

1. Virtual Card Issuance and Reloads

  • Card Issuance Fees are strictly non-refundable once the card number, expiry date, and CVV are generated.
  • Card Reloads/Top-ups: Funds deducted from your USDT Master Wallet for card reloads are held securely until approved by the admin. Once the balance is successfully updated on your Visa/Mastercard network, the transaction is final and cannot be reversed or refunded.

2. Crypto Deposits & Network Errors

  • Wallet deposits are processed via Kripicard. Each deposit gets a unique address on the selected network (e.g., Tron TRC20).
  • Eisy Myanmar is NOT responsible for funds lost due to incorrect network selection, sending to the wrong deposit address, or transferring amounts below the minimum $20.00 USDT threshold. These on-chain transactions cannot be recovered or refunded.

3. P2P Marketplace Disputes

  • In the event of a trade dispute (e.g., fake payment receipts or missing MMK transfers), the escrowed USDT will remain locked. Eisy Myanmar administrators will review the submitted transaction IDs and trade chat logs within 48 hours to make a final, non-negotiable decision on fund release or refund.
