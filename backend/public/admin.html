Rate & Pricing

        

          Five dedicated fee blocks for withdrawals, card issuance, card reloads, USDT deposits, and USDT withdrawals.
          Saves are logged to Exchange Rate History.
        

        

          

            

              

                

                  1Withdraw MMK Rate
                

                
Daily exchange rate for USDT-to-MMK local bank withdrawals (1 USD = X MMK).

              

              

                

                  
                  
                  MMK paid per 1 USDT after withdrawal fee
                

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

            


            

              

                

                  2Card Issue Fee / Rate
                

                
Platform markup fee charged when issuing a new virtual card (kept as platform earnings).

              

              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                  Percent of initial card load (dynamic)
                

                

                  
                  
                

                
Checkout also adds a fixed Processing Fee of $1.50.

              

            


            

              

                

                  3Card Reload Fee (%)
                

                
Card reload fee = max(fixed + percent of top-up, minimum fee). Min USDT reload is the smallest top-up amount.

              

              

                

                  
                  
                  Added to the percentage. Use 0 for percent only.
                

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                  Fee = max(fixed + amount × %, this minimum)
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

            


            

              

                

                  4USDT Deposit Fee / Rate
                

                
Service fee taken from user USDT deposits before net balance is credited. Fee = fixed fee + (deposit × percent / 100).

              

              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

            


            

              

                

                  5USDT Withdrawal Fee
                

                
Withdrawal fee = max(fixed + percent of amount, minimum fee). Payouts are processed within 48 hours.

              

              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                  Fee charged = max(fixed + percent of withdrawal, this minimum)
