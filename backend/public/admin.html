Rate & Pricing

        

          Five dedicated fee blocks for withdrawals, card issuance, card reloads, USDT deposits, and USDT withdrawals.
          Saves are logged to Exchange Rate History.
        

        

          

            

              

                

                  1Withdraw MMK Rate
                

                
Daily exchange rate for USDT-to-MMK local bank withdrawals (1 USD = X MMK).

              

              

                

                  
                  
                  MMK paid per 1 USDT after withdrawal fee
                

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

            


            

              

                

                  2Card Issue Fee / Rate
                

                
Platform markup fee charged when issuing a new virtual card (kept as platform earnings; not sent to Kripicard).

              

              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

            


            

              

                

                  3Card Reload Fee (%)
                

                
Percentage fee applied on top of each card reload amount.

              

              

                

                  
                  
                  Charged as a percent of the top-up amount
                

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

            


            

              

                

                  4USDT Deposit Fee / Rate
                

                
Service fee taken from user USDT deposits before net balance is credited.

              

              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                  Fee charged = max(percent of deposit, this minimum)
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

            


            

              

                

                  5USDT Withdrawal Fee
                

                
Fee for USDT withdrawals: custom percentage with a fixed minimum floor (default $1).

              

              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                  Floor of $1 recommended — fee = max(percent, minimum)
